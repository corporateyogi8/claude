For most of my corporate career, I treated rest like a bonus I'd collect once the deadline was met, once the promotion came through, once things finally calmed down.

They never calmed down. I just got better at running on empty.

The day my body stopped cooperating wasn't dramatic. It was a Tuesday. I was sitting in a meeting I had prepared for all week, and I realised I couldn't remember the last time I had taken a full breath. That was my nervous system sending the invoice for years of pushing through.

What changed things for me wasn't another productivity system or a longer holiday. It was learning to regulate, slow down on purpose and come back into my body. Yin yoga, sound healing, breathwork and honest conversations with women who understood exactly what "high functioning" can cost.

So I've created the space I wish someone had handed me back then.

The CorporateYogi Retreat is for high-achieving women who are brilliant at holding everything together and quietly exhausted from doing it. We'll slow down with sunrise yin yoga, reset with sound healing and nervous system practices, and give you something rare: room to hear yourself think again.

You arrive as the high achiever. You leave as yourself.

It's a deliberately small group so every person is seen and supported. If something in this is stirring for you, comment RETREAT below or send me a message and I'll share the details personally.

Stop surviving. Start living.

## Posting notes

- **Upload the MP4 natively to LinkedIn** instead of linking to it, so it autoplays in the feed. LinkedIn supports 9:16 vertical video and shows it full-screen in its video feed.
- **The first two lines are the hook.** LinkedIn cuts the post off after about 210 characters with "...see more", so the opening needs to carry it.
- **Reply to every "RETREAT" comment within the first hour.** Early engagement boosts reach, and it lets you move the conversation to DMs.
- **Add the retreat details once they're confirmed** (dates, location, price, booking link). Put the link in the first comment rather than the post body, because LinkedIn tends to reduce the reach of posts that contain outbound links.
- **Before posting, check the story in paragraph 3.** The Tuesday meeting moment is a placeholder. Swap in your real moment so the post stays truthful.
- **Re-rendering:** edit `reel-source.html`, then run `node render.js` (requires Playwright). It outputs frames that ffmpeg turns into the MP4.
