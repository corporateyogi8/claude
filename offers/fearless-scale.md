# Fearless Scale: The 90-Day Tech + Mindset Accelerator

*A CorporateYogi offer, built on the 7 P framework of specific messaging.*

---

## 1. Person: Who is it for?

The small business owner (solo founder up to about 10 people, roughly $100K to $1M in revenue) who built something real and is now stuck at the ceiling. They know exactly what they "should" do next: raise prices, hire, automate, show up on video, launch the new offer. But every time they get close, something freezes them. They are the bottleneck of their own business, and deep down they know it.

## 2. Problem: Problems, fears and challenges

**Problems:** Wearing every hat. Working 50 to 60 hours with revenue flat. Drowning in admin, inboxes and manual tasks. A pile of half-set-up tools and apps that never got used.

**Fears:** Being visible and being judged. Raising prices and losing clients. Letting go of control and watching someone else do it "wrong." Investing in tech or a hire and wasting money. Quietly, the fear of success itself, and of being found out as not ready for the next level.

**Challenges:** No time to learn new systems. Overwhelm about which tools (and which AI) actually matter. Past attempts at scaling that fizzled. Decisions made from stress and scarcity instead of clarity.

## 3. Promise: Where do they want to go, and by when?

In 90 days, clear the emotional blocks that keep you playing small, get back 10+ hours a week through simple tech and automation, and finally take the one growth move you have been avoiding, whether that is a price increase, a first hire, a launch or getting visible.

## 4. Path: How will they get there?

Three phases, 30 days each:

| Phase | Days | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| **Uncover** | 1 to 30 | Fear audit plus business operations audit. Find exactly where emotion is blocking growth and where time is leaking. Learn nervous system regulation so decisions come from calm, not panic. |
| **Unblock** | 31 to 60 | Coaching through the core fears: money, visibility, control and delegation. Set up the tech stack: CRM, scheduling, email automation and AI assistants for content and admin. |
| **Scale** | 61 to 90 | Hand off tasks with SOPs, run the business on systems instead of willpower, and execute the growth move with support. Leave with a Scale Scorecard to keep going. |
