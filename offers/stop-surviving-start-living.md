## 1. Person: Who is it for?

A high-achieving woman, roughly 35 to 50, in a mid-to-senior corporate role. From the outside she has it all: the title, the salary, the reputation as the one who always delivers. On the inside she is running on caffeine and cortisol. She has tried the meditation app, the long weekend away and the "just set better boundaries" advice, and none of it stuck. She is a little skeptical of anything that sounds too "woo," but she knows something has to change.

## 2. Problem: Problems, fears and challenges

**Problems:** She feels wired but tired. She can't switch off at night, wakes at 3am running through her inbox, snaps at her partner and kids, and feels like she has lost the woman she used to be.

**Fears:** That slowing down will cost her her edge or her next promotion. That this exhaustion is simply who she is now. That if she stops holding everything together, it will all fall apart.

**Challenges:** No spare time. A calendar that isn't hers. Skepticism about whether "wellness" actually works for someone like her. Not knowing where to start.

## 3. Promise: Where does she want to go, and by when?

In 8 weeks, move from surviving on adrenaline to feeling calm, clear and back in control, without quitting your job. Sleep through the night, switch off when the laptop closes, hold boundaries without guilt, and show up at home as the woman you actually want to be.
