# The Fearless Founders Circle

*A free weekly community by CorporateYogi. The front door to Fearless Scale.*

**Tagline:** Grow your business without the fear, the overwhelm or doing it alone.

---

## The 7 Ps at a glance

| P | Fearless Founders Circle |
|---|---|
| **Person** | Small business owners and solo founders who feel stuck, overwhelmed by tech, or held back by fear of visibility, pricing or delegation. |
| **Problem** | Isolation, decision fatigue, tech overwhelm and the quiet fears no one talks about in business groups. |
| **Promise** | Every week, leave with one calm, clear brave move and one tech shortcut you can use straight away. |
| **Path** | Show up weekly, take one brave move, share the win. Small steps, every week, with people who get it. |
| **Packaging** | Free weekly live call, community space, tool-of-the-week library, weekly rituals. |
| **Proof** | Weekly wins thread, member spotlights, Kim's story. |
| **Pricepoint** | Free. Value is delivered upfront, and members who want deeper support are invited into Fearless Scale. |

---

## The weekly live call (60 minutes, same day and time every week)

| Time | Segment | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 5 min | **Ground** | A 3-minute guided breath to shift from busy mode to clear mode. |
| 5 to 20 min | **Teach** | One short lesson, following the monthly rotation below. |
| 20 to 45 min | **Hot seats** | 2 to 3 members bring a real block, fear or tech problem and get coached live. |
| 45 to 55 min | **Brave Move** | Everyone types their one brave move for the week into the chat. |
| 55 to 60 min | **Close** | Celebrate, plus a light mention of what is opening up next (Fearless Scale, workshops). |

## Monthly rotation

| Week | Theme | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Fear Lab** | "Why raising your prices feels dangerous, and how to calm your body before you do it" |
| 2 | **Tech Shortcut** | Live demo: an AI prompt that writes your client follow-up emails in 5 minutes |
| 3 | **Hot Seat Week** | Extended coaching, more members get the floor |
| 4 | **Wins and Rhythm** | Celebrate the month's brave moves, plus a reset for the month ahead |

## Community rituals (between calls)

- **Monday Brave Move thread:** post your one step for the week
- **Wednesday Tool Drop:** Kim shares one tool, template or AI prompt
- **Friday Wins thread:** share what you did, however small
- **Monthly member spotlight:** one member's journey, shared in the group and (with permission) on Instagram

## Platform

**Recommended: Skool** (free group with a built-in calendar, classroom for replays and the Tool Drop library, and a leaderboard to encourage engagement). Zoom for the live calls, with replays posted within 24 hours.

*Alternatives:* a Facebook Group (easy to reach people, but more distractions) or Circle (polished, but paid).

## Member journey

1. **Join** through the link in your Instagram bio, a lead magnet or a referral.
2. **Welcome:** an automated DM and email with a 2-minute welcome video, the call calendar link and a prompt to introduce yourself.
3. **First call:** a personal shout-out to new members, plus an invitation to post their first Brave Move.
4. **Engaged member:** keeps showing up, posts wins, takes part in hot seats.
5. **Invitation:** once a quarter, a 3-day "Scale Clarity" mini-workshop inside the community leads into a cart opening for Fearless Scale. Members can also book a free Scale Clarity Call at any time.

## Community guidelines

1. Confidential: what is shared in the Circle stays in the Circle.
2. Encourage, don't advise unless asked.
3. No pitching or self-promotion outside the monthly promo thread.
4. Every win counts, especially the small ones.

## 2-week launch plan

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 to 2 | Set up Skool, the welcome video, the welcome automation and the guidelines. |
| 3 to 5 | Instagram teaser series: "Something free is coming for founders who are tired of doing it alone." |
| 6 | Open the doors, with a founding member post and a link in bio. |
| 7 to 13 | Daily stories and a personal DM invite to 20 warm contacts. Goal: 30 to 50 founding members. |
| 14 | First live call: the "Fear Lab: What's really stopping you from scaling?" session. |

## What to track

- Members joined each week
- Live call attendance (target: 25% or more of active members)
- Brave Moves and wins posted each week
- Scale Clarity Calls booked
- Conversion to Fearless Scale (target: 5 to 10% of engaged members per cart opening)
